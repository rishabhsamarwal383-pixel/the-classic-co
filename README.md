# THE CLASSIC CO. — Architectural Eyewear Studio · Udaipur

High-converting luxury sunglasses e-commerce storefront & local 45-minute delivery engine with automated Google Sheets CRM, customer confirmation emails, owner alerts, and WhatsApp order telemetry.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Architectural Eyewear Catalog (32 Silhouettes):**
   - High-definition Shopify CDN imagery with multi-image thumbnail previews on cards.
   - Categories: 🧲 4-in-1 Magnetic Snap-Ons, 🕶️ 9-Layer TAC Polarized & Driving, ✨ Retro Trends, 💎 Luxury Acetate.
   - Live real-time search with clear button and sorting (Featured, Price: Low-to-High, High-to-Low, Bestsellers).

2. **Frictionless Dual-Pricing Checkout:**
   - ⚡ **Instant UPI Special @ ₹550** (Save ₹448 vs COD) with dynamic NPCI UPI QR code, deep links for Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED, and 1-tap UPI ID copy.
   - 💵 **Cash on Delivery (₹998 total)** with Udaipur doorstep mirror trial (try before paying).
   - Form inputs with validation: Full Name, Email ID, 10-digit WhatsApp Number, Address, Landmark, 6-digit Pincode, and GPS Live Map Pin locator.

3. **Automated Google Sheets CRM & Email Engine:**
   - Orders are dispatched via Google Apps Script Web App.
   - Every order logs into the **"Orders"** Google Sheet with full customer and order details.
   - **Automated Customer Email:** Instant luxury HTML confirmation email with itemized invoice, 45-min dispatch ETA, and WhatsApp support.
   - **Automated Owner Alert:** Instant notification to store owner with 1-tap call & WhatsApp links.
   - Custom Google Sheets Operations menu: mark dispatched (auto-emails customer), mark delivered, open WhatsApp chat.

4. **Live Order Tracking Modal:**
   - Customers can enter their Order ID or WhatsApp number anytime to view the live 4-step dispatch status and arrival ETA.

5. **Local Social Proof & Trust:**
   - Verified buyer reviews from Udaipur patrons.
   - Interactive FAQ accordion answering top questions about Doorstep Mirror Trial, Polarized UV400 lenses, and delivery times.
   - Floating WhatsApp Support button (+91 8619661325).
